## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a calm, pristine, and effortless personal finance tracking experience designed specifically for mobile-first consumer interaction. Drawing from modern minimalism and tactile digital clarity, the interface relies on vast white breathing room, high-contrast structural text, and friendly geometric shapes to transform money management from an anxiety-inducing chore into an intuitive, lightweight routine.

### Creative Tenets
- **Airy Precision:** Clean canvases layered with featherweight structural lines rather than heavy boxes.
- **Graphic Directness:** Deep monochromatic accents grounded by crisp, colorful telemetry rings and status nodes.
- **Soft Touch Ergonomics:** High physical compliance through fully rounded pill controls and generous interactive hit-targets.
- **Clarity Over Density:** Low cognitive load achieved by surfacing primary totals boldly while stepping metadata down to soft secondary tones.

## Colors

The palette employs a high-contrast monochrome foundation accented by purposeful semantic anchors. Contrast is deployed intentionally: deep slate inks convey finality and value, soft off-whites delineate ambient card regions, and hairline neutrals provide structure without visual clutter.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#121316`):** Key interactive buttons, prominent summaries, and primary active states. Content on top must strictly use `#FFFFFF`.
- **Primary Soft (`#F1F2F5`):** Subdued pill backgrounds, pressed states, and low-priority active indicators.
- **Secondary (`#5D5E61`):** Navigational icons, descriptive subheaders, and contextual units.
- **Tertiary (`#AAABAE`):** Inactive indicators, trailing hints, and placeholder text.
- **Background & Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** Base canvas and main interactive modules.
- **Surface 2 (`#F3F5F7`):** Recessed containers, telemetry backplates, segmented control tracks, and input beds.
- **Ink (`#181A1C`):** High-prominence typography, balances, and transactional figures.
- **Ink 2 (`#66686D`):** Standard body copy, timestamps, and secondary balance changes.
- **Line (`#E2E4E7`):** Hairline borders (1px) for card structures, list dividers, and concentric track rings.
- **Inverse (`#1C1E20`):** Floating navigation pills, tooltips, and high-impact snackbars.

### Semantic Accents
- **Success (`#159A49`):** Positive cash flow, completed goals, and income metrics.
- **Warning (`#C97D00`):** Approaching budget limits, scheduled outflows, and alerts.
- **Danger (`#D32F34`):** Over-budget notifications, account disconnects, and destructive actions.

## Typography

The typography uses Plus Jakarta Sans to manifest the friendly, modern, and open geometry required by the Breeze aesthetic. Metrics and monetary values favor bold, tight-tracking weights for quick recognition, while supporting labels balance high legibility with subdued hierarchy.

### Rules of Usage
- **Financial Balance Rules:** Use `display-lg` and `display-md` strictly for top-level wallet and net-worth metrics. Keep decimal segments optically equal or stepped down one level to preserve baseline stability.
- **Labels & Micro-Copy:** Use `label-sm` set to uppercase with wide letter spacing for category trackers, concentric ring telemetry tags, and table metadata.
- **Icon Pairing:** Material Symbols Outlined must match optical font sizing (e.g., 20px glyphs with `label-lg` or `body-md`, 24px glyphs with `headline-lg` or `title-md`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around mobile-first utility, using an edge-to-edge container model bounded by consistent viewport margins. Spatial grouping relies on progressive whitespace stacks rather than heavy panel boundaries.

### Grid & Boundaries
- **Mobile Foundation:** Single-column modular flow bounded by an outer margin of `1.25rem` (20px). Touch targets maintain an absolute minimum height of 48px.
- **Gaps & Rhythm:** Inner card components leverage `space-md` (16px) for item grouping, while major contextual sections (e.g., Chart Stack to Transaction Feed) separate via `space-xl` (32px).
- **Tablet Reflow:** For larger viewports, the layout pins to a 480px max-width centered container to maintain thumb ergonomics, or splits into a two-column balance sheet layout with a 1rem gutter.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects layered heavy drop shadows in favor of crisp 1px hairline borders (`#E2E4E7`) paired with ambient, diffused off-surface blurs.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Base Level (Canvas):** Pure `#FFFFFF` flat plane.
- **Recessed Level:** `#F3F5F7` with no shadow, cut with a 1px border of `#E2E4E7`. Used for segmented tabs, search fields, and metric tracks.
- **Card Level:** Surface `#FFFFFF` enclosed by a 1px border (`#E2E4E7`) and an ambient glow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(18, 19, 22, 0.04)`.
- **Floating Overlays & Sheets:** Deep floating pill navbars and sticky actions utilize `#1C1E20` or translucent frosted glass (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) supported by `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(18, 19, 22, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The interface expresses its soft, consumer-first personality through extreme corner softening. Elements alternate between high-radius organic rectangles and complete rounded pills.

### Geometry Rules
- **Pills (`rounded-full`):** All interactive buttons, filter chips, category capsules, and bottom floating toolbars use complete 9999px pill radii.
- **Cards & Data Shields:** Standard overview cards, charts, and modal sheets adopt `rounded-2xl` (1rem / 16px to 1.5rem / 24px) to retain structural alignment without harsh corners.
- **Concentric Data Rings:** Circular visualizers, budget meters, and transaction progress indicators must be modeled with perfect concentric circular geometry, maintaining equal stroke-to-radius intervals.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill:** Background `#121316`, label `#FFFFFF` (`label-lg`), height 48px, padding 0 24px, radius `rounded-full`. Active scale down to 0.98.
- **Secondary Pill:** Background `#F1F2F5`, label `#181A1C`, 1px border `#E2E4E7`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** Transparent background, label `#5D5E61`, active state renders `#F3F5F7`.

### Chips & Filters
- Compact 32px pills. Unselected: `#F3F5F7` background, `#66686D` text. Selected: `#121316` background, `#FFFFFF` text. Icon prefix sized to 16px.

### Cards
- Container filled with `#FFFFFF`, bounded by 1px border `#E2E4E7`, corners `rounded-2xl`, inner padding `space-lg` (24px).
- Internal sections are separated by full-width 1px hairline dividers (`#E2E4E7`).

### Concentric Data Rings (Budgets & Spending)
- Outer tracking rings use stroke width of 8px to 12px.
- Unfilled background track uses `#F3F5F7`.
- Primary ring paths use `#121316`, `#159A49`, `#C97D00`, or `#D32F34` with rounded cap terminations (`stroke-linecap: round`).
- Spacing between nested concentric rings must remain fixed at 4px.

### Lists & Transactions
- Edge-to-edge content blocks separated by 1px `#E2E4E7` borders (indented to match label text).
- Left-hand category avatar: 40px circle or `rounded-xl` square in `#F3F5F7`, housing a 20px Material Symbols Outlined glyph in `#181A1C`.
- Primary title (`title-md`) in `#181A1C`, metadata (`body-md`) in `#66686D`.
- Right-aligned transactional amount rendered in `title-md` (`#181A1C` for expense, `#159A49` for income).

### Input Fields
- Height 52px, filled with `#F3F5F7`, 1px hairline border `#E2E4E7`, radius `rounded-xl`.
- Focus state shifts border to `#121316` with zero focus ring spread. Text in `#181A1C`, placeholder in `#AAABAE`.

### Checkboxes & Toggle Switches
- Checkbox: 20px box with `rounded-md`, 1.5px border `#AAABAE`, checked background `#121316` displaying white check icon.
- Switch: 48px x 28px pill track in `#E2E4E7`, active track `#121316`. Thumb is 22px pure `#FFFFFF` circle.
